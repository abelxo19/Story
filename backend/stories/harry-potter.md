## The Letter

![](/images/stories/harry-potter/scene-1.svg)

A boy grew up in a house that did not want him. He slept in a cupboard, wore clothes that did not fit, and learned to stay quiet. On an ordinary morning, letters began to arrive — more letters than a cupboard child was supposed to receive.

They named a school he had never heard of, and a world that had been waiting for him. When a giant friend finally came to fetch him, the boy learned two astonishing facts: his parents had loved him, and he was a wizard.

## The Hidden Platform

![](/images/stories/harry-potter/scene-2.svg)

At a crowded station, the way forward was not a door but a barrier between trains. The boy ran at it, heart thudding, and the ordinary world folded away. A scarlet train carried him toward towers, lakes, and a school that lived half in story and half in stone.

On the journey he found his first true friends: one who talked as if words were fireworks, and one who was loyal before he was sure. Together they watched the countryside turn into magic.

## The Great Hall

![](/images/stories/harry-potter/scene-3.svg)

Under a ceiling full of candles, a talking hat searched the boy's thoughts. It spoke of choices, not only of talent, and sent him to a table where courage was a house and a home. Feast, song, and starlight followed.

In the weeks after, he learned that school could hold both friendship and danger. Brooms flew. Paintings argued. A three-headed mystery slept behind a locked door, and curiosity — that old companion of children — would not leave it alone.

## First Courage

![](/images/stories/harry-potter/scene-4.svg)

When the year asked more of him than lessons, the boy did not stand alone. His friends walked with him into the dark under the school, through puzzles, fear, and a truth about the past. He discovered that love can leave a mark stronger than a curse.

He returned to the summer house changed. The cupboard was no longer his place. The next train, he knew, would come again — and with it, more of the life that had been waiting since the first letter found him.
